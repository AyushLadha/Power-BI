# 📊 Business Intelligence Dashboards — Power BI

A collection of interactive Power BI dashboards delivering executive-level insights across customer analytics, revenue performance, product tracking, and global sales distribution.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates end-to-end business intelligence design — from raw data modeling to polished, decision-ready dashboards. Each report is built to serve a specific stakeholder audience, mimicking the kind of BI work delivered to finance and sales leadership in enterprise settings.

**What this project covers:**
- Dimensional data modeling and DAX measure development
- Multi-page report design with cross-filter interactivity
- KPI tracking against targets with trend analysis
- Geographic sales visualization across global markets

---

## 📁 Dashboards

### 1. 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer Dashboard
> *Who are our most valuable customers, and how is behavior shifting over time?*

- Customer demographics breakdown
- Top customers ranked by revenue contribution
- Customer acquisition and retention trends over time
- Segment-level behavioral patterns

---

### 2. 📈 Executive Dashboard
> *A single-pane view of overall business health for senior leadership.*

- KPI cards: Total Revenue, Total Profit, Order Volume, Return Rate
- Revenue and orders trend lines (monthly/quarterly)
- Period-over-period variance analysis
- High-level performance indicators for quarterly planning

---

### 3. 🌍 Geographical Analysis
> *Where are we winning — and where is the opportunity?*

- Sales distribution across major global regions
- Market strength heatmap by geography
- Region-level revenue and order volume comparison
- Identification of underperforming or high-growth markets

---

### 4. 📦 Product Details
> *Deep-dive performance tracking at the product level.*

- Orders vs. targets — actual vs. goal comparison
- Monthly revenue and profitability trends by product
- Return rate tracking per product line
- Margin analysis to identify high- and low-performing SKUs
